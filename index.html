Our Philosophy

Building a Strong Foundation for Lifelong Success

Ridoana Comprehensive School (often referred to as Ridoana School Complex) is a private educational institution located in Atadeka, Ashaiman in the Greater Accra Region of Ghana. We merge rigorous academic study with vital character training, ensuring children grow into confident, capable, and responsible citizens.

Our dedicated faculty partners closely with parents to maintain a transparent, supportive, and stimulating atmosphere where learning thrives every single day.
